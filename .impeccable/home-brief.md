# Surface: public home page (resources/js/pages/home.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: clinic owners watching a sales demo, playing to prospective patients in Metro Manila and Cebu. Action: book a consultation. Proof: real treatments, prices, practitioners, branches from the database; a booking that lands in the admin.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a concierge writing your consultation onto an appointment card; the booking card is the real control, not decoration. Refuses the category default of a model-portrait hero with a beige treatment grid.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest #173B35 fields and warm ivory #F7F3EC card stock, white card faces, ink #18201E. Champagne #C8A96B only as hairline foil rules and one glow. Square corners, 1px hairlines, debossed (inset shadow) card edges, Instrument Sans display, Geist body, tabular numerals.

STORY: Visitor sees the offer, starts a card in the hero (treatment, branch), scrolls through treatments, specialists, locations and trust, and every section can drop its item onto the card; the card follows them to booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed forest. Left 5/12: headline "A more intelligent approach to aesthetic care.", support line, primary "Book a consultation", secondary "Explore treatments". Right 7/12: 3D sculpture (poster/video on mobile) with the ivory appointment card overlapping its lower-left edge, showing treatment, branch and next open times as large numerals.

FORM: The Appointment Card, candidate 7 of 7, seed key 8680a080. Raises: one light source (indoor sun), punched-mark progress rail (select rail), live branch open state (city fold), remembered card (minihompy), giant numerals (illuminated initial).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
