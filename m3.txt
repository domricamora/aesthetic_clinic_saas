Let the office change a treatment photo, and put the catalogue in the menu

Two things, one of them a bug nobody had noticed.

The whole catalogue was unreachable from the sidebar. Products and
treatments existed, were editable, and could only be got to by typing the
url. A clinic owner's first question about this software is what it sells,
and the answer was not in the menu.

And a treatment photograph, which is most of what a treatments page is, had
no way to be changed from the office. The column was seeded with filenames
and nothing could set it. It is uploaded, stored as WebP like every other
image here, and removable, behind the same permission as the rest of the
catalogue.

The sidebar is now ordered the way the day runs rather than by module:
bookings, then the people asking about them, then what is sold, then the
shelf, then the people who do the work, then the books, then settings. The
seeding and reset tools move below a divider, because they are for setting
up a demonstration rather than for running a clinic, and a real clinic
should not have to look past them every day.
