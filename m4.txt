Write down who did what

A refund, a stock correction, a payroll run approved or paid: each of those
moves money or trust, and none of them left a trace. An operator with a
discrepancy had nothing to look at and the person being asked about it had
nothing to answer with.

Entries are written by the action that did the thing rather than by
middleware watching for changes. A watcher can only say what the database
looks like afterwards; the action knows what it refused, what it charged
and why, and that is the part worth keeping.

The screen is deliberately read-only. There is no write route at all, because
a log somebody can tidy up is not evidence of anything, and "who did this"
has to have the same answer next month as it has today. Retention is left as
a scheduled prune rather than a button, on purpose.

Staff actions record the address they came from. Recording where a member of
the public came from is a different question with different consent, and
that belongs on a lead rather than here.
