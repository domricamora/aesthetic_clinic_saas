Take the website roster from the office, not from a seeder

The about page listed a roster that nobody could change. The same people
existed twice -- as seeded specialist rows, and as staff records an
administrator can edit -- and the page preferred the seeded copy and
filtered the editable one out by name. A title, a photograph or a line of
copy corrected in the office simply never reached the site.

The staff roll is now the source of truth. Only somebody with "show on
the website" ticked appears, a resigned person appears nowhere, and the
seeded specialists are kept only for somebody who is not on the payroll at
all -- a visiting doctor, say -- who would otherwise have nowhere to be
listed.

The demo data ticks the clinicians, so the site has a roster to show. The
flag still defaults to off, because a name and a photograph going live by
accident is not something anybody wants to hear about from a customer.
