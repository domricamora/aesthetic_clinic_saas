The desk finds out a message arrived without pressing Refresh

A chat that only appears when somebody reloads is not much of a chat. A visitor
types a question, and the desk is told nothing until someone happens to land
on the page again. Three things fix that.

The inbox and the open thread poll a JSON endpoint and merge what comes back.
The sidebar carries a count, so the badge appears whichever screen the desk is
actually on. A new row gets a brief "new" marker rather than silently
appearing at the top of a list somebody is reading.

Auto-scroll is deliberately conditional. It follows a visitor's message only
if the reader was already at the bottom, and never follows the desk's own
replies -- jumping the page under somebody mid-sentence is worse than making
them scroll.

Polling rather than websockets, and the reason is worth writing down: the
clinic runs on a plain PHP host with no long-lived process to hold a socket
open. A timer over a table that changes when a visitor types covers this. The
hook pauses on a hidden tab, catches up on return, and never overlaps
requests, so a closed laptop is not quietly reloading an inbox all afternoon.

The inbox rows also label who spoke last. A preview reading "Yes, before 5pm"
is ambiguous until you know which of you said it, and that ambiguity is the
difference between a conversation waiting on you and one already answered.

The feed sits behind the same permission as the inbox. It is a second way to
read the same conversations, so gating one and not the other would leak them.
