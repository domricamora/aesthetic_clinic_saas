Put a chat on the website that reaches the front desk

A visitor with a question had the phone number of a clinic and nothing
else. The enquiries that did arrive came from forms, and somebody who
wanted to ask one question was not going to fill one in.

The window identifies a visitor by a token in their own browser rather than
an account, because a sign-up wall in front of the question loses the
question. Nothing is sent until somebody types, so loading a page does not
create a conversation somebody then has to close.

The part that matters is what happens next. The moment a visitor gives a
name and a number, the conversation becomes a lead with the consent
timestamp on it, so it lands in the same queue as a form submission and the
desk can actually call somebody back. It happens once, however many further
messages arrive, or the queue fills with the same person.

A token only ever grants the ability to write to that one thread. The rate
limit is per token rather than per address, so a clinic in a shared office
does not lock every visitor behind one outgoing at once.

The office turns it on and sets the greeting in Clinic settings. It is off
until they do: a chat nobody answers is worse than no chat, because the
visitor has been told to expect a reply.
