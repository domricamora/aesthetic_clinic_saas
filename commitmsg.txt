Let every employee have their own statutory rate

The clinic sets one SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG rate, and it is right for
most of the staff roll. It is not right for all of it: a part-time
therapist under the first bracket, a consultant whose PhilHealth is
arranged another way, somebody whose rate changed part-way through the
year. There was nowhere to record that, so the payroll run quietly
produced the wrong figure for them and nobody could tell it was wrong.

Withholding was worse: it lived in a config file and could not be changed
from the office at all, let alone per person.

Each of the four is now a nullable column on the employee, and blank means
the clinic rate. Null rather than zero on purpose -- a blank box is a
decision to defer to the clinic, and a zero would read as "stop
deducting" and quietly pay somebody the wrong way. An employee who has
never been touched therefore behaves exactly as before, with no history to
reconcile.

Tax exemption is a checkbox rather than a rate of zero, because exempt is
the decision and zero is an artefact. It also wins over any rate left
behind.

Rates apply going forward, as everywhere else in payroll: a payslip
already calculated keeps the figure it was paid.
