Round the chat, and shrink it to a button

The expanded panel was a square-cornered box that read as a piece of the page
rather than something floating on top of it, and the closed state was a wide
labelled pill sitting over the footer. Now the panel is rounded and lifted,
and the closed state is a circle with just the icon.

The label moves to aria-label rather than disappearing: a screen reader was
getting the word "Chat" from the text, and it still does.

Rounded here and square everywhere else is deliberate rather than a slip --
this floats over the page, and so should look like it.
